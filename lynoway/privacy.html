Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 9, 2026

1. Introduction

Lynoway ("the App") respects and protects the privacy of all users. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information.

The App is a focus and Pomodoro timer tool. It only accesses necessary device permissions, and all data is stored locally on your device only, never uploaded to any server.

2. Information We Collect

The App does not collect, store, or upload any personally identifiable information (PII).

We only temporarily access the following data when you actively use related features:

  • Custom Background Music: Only when you choose to import local audio files, we temporarily access your device media library to load your selected files. Files are never uploaded or shared.
  • Custom Background Images: Only when you choose to import local images, we temporarily access your device photo library to load your selected images. Images are never uploaded or shared.
  • Focus Statistics: Your focus duration and history are stored only on your device locally and are never synced or uploaded to any external server.

3. Data Storage and Usage

All user data (including focus records, custom backgrounds, and music files) is stored only on your device. We cannot access, read, or modify this data.

We do not sell, trade, or transfer any of your data to third parties.

4. Device Permissions

To enable core features, the App only requests the following necessary permissions:

  • Media/Photo Library Access: To allow you to select custom background music and images. You can revoke this permission at any time in your device settings. Revoking it will disable custom media features but will not affect basic timer functionality.
  • Background App Refresh: To keep the Pomodoro timer running in the background. No other data is accessed.
  • Notifications: To send reminders when focus sessions end (optional, you can disable this at any time).

5. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. No information is sent to any third party.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected data from a child, please contact us at the address below and we will delete it immediately.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Revoke permissions for the App at any time in your device settings
  • Delete the App, which will remove all local data associated with it
  • Contact us to inquire about or correct your information (we hold no data, so we can only confirm no collection)

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 6 月 9 日

1. 引言

Lynoway（以下简称 "本应用"）尊重并保护所有用户的隐私。本隐私政策旨在说明我们如何收集、使用、存储和保护您的信息。

本应用是一款专注类番茄钟工具，仅在必要时访问您的设备权限，所有数据均仅存储在您的本地设备中，不上传至服务器。

2. 我们收集的信息

本应用不收集、不存储、不上传任何个人身份信息（PII）。

仅在您主动使用以下功能时，才会临时访问相关数据：

  • 自定义背景音乐：仅在您选择导入本地音频文件时，临时读取您的设备媒体库，以加载您选择的音频文件。文件不会被上传或分享。
  • 自定义背景图片：仅在您选择导入本地图片时，临时读取您的设备相册，以加载您选择的图片。图片不会被上传或分享。
  • 专注数据统计：您的专注时长、历史记录等数据仅存储在您的设备本地，不会同步或上传至任何外部服务器。

3. 数据存储与使用

所有用户数据（包括专注记录、自定义背景 / 音乐文件）仅保存在您的设备本地，我们无法访问、读取或修改这些数据。

我们不会将您的任何数据出售、交易或转让给第三方。

4. 设备权限说明

为实现核心功能，本应用仅请求以下必要权限：

  • 媒体库 / 相册访问权限：用于您选择自定义背景音乐和背景图片。您可以随时在系统设置中关闭该权限，关闭后将无法使用自定义媒体功能，但不影响基础计时功能。
  • 后台运行权限：用于在后台保持番茄钟计时，不会访问或使用您的其他数据。
  • 通知权限：用于在专注结束时发送提醒通知（可选，您可随时关闭）。

5. 第三方服务

本应用不集成任何第三方统计、广告或分析 SDK，因此不会向任何第三方发送您的信息。

6. 未成年人隐私
