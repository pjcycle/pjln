Frequently Asked Questions

1. How do I start a focus session?

Open Lynoway, choose a duration (e.g., 25 minutes), then tap the start button. You can also start a Pomodoro round directly from the Home screen.

2. Where is my focus data stored?

All focus sessions and tags are saved locally on your device only. Lynoway does not upload any data to a remote server. See the Privacy Policy for details.

3. How do I import custom background music or images?

Go to Settings → Theme → Custom, then pick a file from your local library. Lynoway only reads the file you select; nothing is uploaded or shared.

4. Does Lynoway have a subscription?

Yes. An optional subscription unlocks extended statistics, additional themes, and premium soundscapes. A free trial may be offered to new subscribers; you can cancel anytime in your Apple ID Subscriptions settings. See the User Agreement for full details.

5. How do I delete all my local data?

Open Settings → Data → "Clear All" to wipe local focus records and settings. Uninstalling the App also removes all locally stored data.

6. Notifications aren't firing at the end of a session.

Check iOS Settings → Notifications → Lynoway, and make sure notifications are allowed. Also confirm Focus / Do Not Disturb modes are not suppressing alerts.

7. Can I use Lynoway offline?

Yes. Lynoway runs entirely on-device and does not require an internet connection for core timer and recording features.

常见问题

1. 如何开始一次专注？

打开 Lynoway，选择一个时长（如 25 分钟），然后点击开始按钮。您也可以在首页直接启动一轮番茄钟。

2. 我的专注数据存储在哪里？

所有专注记录与标签仅保存在您的设备本地，Lynoway 不会上传任何数据到远程服务器。详情请见 隐私政策。

3. 如何导入自定义背景音乐或图片？

进入「设置 → 主题 → 自定义」，从本地媒体库中选择文件即可。Lynoway 仅读取您选择的文件，不会上传或分享。

4. Lynoway 是否包含订阅？

是的。可选订阅可解锁扩展统计、更多主题与高级背景音。新订阅用户可能享受免费试用，您可随时在 Apple ID「订阅」设置中取消。详情请见 使用条款。

5. 如何删除所有本地数据？

进入「设置 → 数据 → 全部清除」即可清空本地专注记录与设置。卸载本应用同样会移除所有本地数据。

6. 专注结束的通知没有弹出？

请检查 iOS「设置 → 通知 → Lynoway」，确认已允许通知；同时确认「专注模式 / 勿扰模式」没有屏蔽提醒。

7. 能否离线使用 Lynoway？

可以。Lynoway 完全在设备本地运行，核心计时与记录功能无需网络连接。
