Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

Support: https://pjcycle.github.io/pjln/support

  • Local Desktop Tool
    DemoFlow is a macOS local desktop tool. We do not operate any of our own servers to collect screen recordings, media, screen content, camera, or microphone content.
  • You Choose Where Files Are Saved
    Files you export are written to the location you select in the standard macOS save dialog. DemoFlow does not write exports into its own sandbox container.
  • macOS Permissions You Control
    Screen recording, camera, microphone, and system audio features require you to grant the corresponding macOS permissions. You can revoke them at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  • Local Records
    App settings, output directory bookmarks, subscription status, and one-time 7-day experience records are stored on your local device only.
  • Anonymous Install ID & Trial Time
    An anonymous installation identifier and trial-time record are stored in the macOS Keychain. Neither is derived from hardware information, nor uploaded by DemoFlow.
  • Purchases via Apple StoreKit
    Purchases and purchase restoration are processed through Apple StoreKit and the App Store. Apple handles transaction information according to its own privacy policies.
  • URL Audio Extraction
    The URL audio extraction feature only accesses the network when you actively provide a URL.
  • Language and Region
    We read your system language and region only to choose between the Chinese and English interface; they are never used for any other purpose.
  • Local Diagnostic Logs
    Diagnostic logs are stored locally on your device and are not automatically uploaded.
  • Uninstall Behavior
    Uninstalling the app may leave Keychain records behind, and never deletes files you have exported to your self-selected directories.
  • Privacy Contact
    For privacy questions, contact us at pjln.top@gmail.com.

隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 9 月 4 日

使用条款 (EULA)： https://pjcycle.github.io/pjln/userAgreement

隐私政策： https://pjcycle.github.io/pjln/

支持： https://pjcycle.github.io/pjln/support

  • 本地桌面工具
    DemoFlow 是一款 macOS 本地桌面工具。我们不运营任何自有服务器来收集录屏、媒体、屏幕内容、摄像头或麦克风内容。
  • 保存位置由您选择
    您导出的文件会写入您通过标准 macOS 保存对话框自行选择的位置。DemoFlow 不会将导出文件写入自身的沙盒目录。
  • 由您掌控的 macOS 权限
    使用录屏、摄像头、麦克风和系统音频功能时，需要您授予 macOS 对应的权限。您可随时在「系统设置 → 隐私与安全性」中撤销这些授权。
  • 本地记录
    应用设置、输出目录书签、订阅状态以及一次性 7 天体验记录均仅保存在您的本机。
  • 匿名安装标识与试用时间
    匿名安装标识与试用时间记录保存在 macOS 钥匙串中，既不由硬件信息派生，也不会由 DemoFlow 上传。
  • 通过 Apple StoreKit 购买
    购买和恢复购买通过 Apple StoreKit 与 App Store 完成。Apple 按其自身的隐私政策处理交易信息。
  • URL 音频提取
    URL 音频提取功能仅在您主动提供 URL 时才会访问网络。
  • 语言与地区
    我们仅读取系统语言和地区用于中英文界面切换，不作他用。
  • 本地诊断日志
    诊断日志保存在本机，不自动上传。
  • 卸载行为
    卸载应用可能会保留钥匙串中的记录，也不会删除您已导出至自选位置的文件。
  • 隐私联系
    如有隐私相关问题，请通过 pjln.top@gmail.com 与我们联系。
