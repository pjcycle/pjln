Privacy Policy for PJFlow

Last updated: 2026-05-27

PJFlow is a screen recording application that runs entirely on your local device.

Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or share any personal user data. All recordings and settings are saved locally on your device only.

Permissions Usage

  • Microphone access is only used to record audio along with your screen. Audio data is processed locally and never sent to external servers.
  • Camera access is only used for picture-in-picture preview during recording. Camera data is processed locally and never sent to external servers.
  • Screen recording access is only used to capture your screen content for local recordings.
